# Hex Tile Image Requirements

To ensure your custom PNG images fit perfectly within the hex tiles:

- **Image Size:** Tiles must be a perfect square (e.g., 256x256 or 512x512 pixels).
- **Hex Placement:** The hexagon should be centered within the image, with transparent corners.
- **Transparency:** Use a transparent background outside the hex shape to avoid unwanted artifacts.
- **No Cropping/Splitting:** If your image appears split or cropped, check that it is square and the hex is centered.

**Recommended Template:**
- Start with a 256x256 PNG.
- Draw your hexagon centered, leaving equal transparent space on all sides.

This ensures your tile will render cleanly and align with the grid.
