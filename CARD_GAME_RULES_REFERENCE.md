## 1. SETUP & GOAL

### Before You Start

**You need:**
- 4 Wayfarer cards (one per player, or 2 per solo player)
- 20 Chapter cards (shuffled in order or randomized)
- Dice: d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, d20
- Paper and pencil for tracking
- Optional: Monster cards

**Setup (2 minutes):**
1. Each player picks a Wayfarer card.
2. Place all 20 Chapter cards face-down in a deck.
3. Place Wayfarer cards in front of each player where everyone can see.
4. Give each Wayfarer a piece of paper to track:
   - Current Stress (out of their Stress Limit)
   - Trauma (0-6 maximum per Wayfarer)
   - Items held (max 2-3 depending on Wayfarer)
   - Active conditions (Waterlogged, Isolated, etc.)
5. Shuffle the loot deck (or draw from the loot list as needed).

### The Goal

**Win Condition:**
Survive the entire 20-card deck (or a shorter deck if playing fast). Reach Card 20 and pass the final test.

**Lose Condition:**
If all Wayfarers are dead (each has 6 Trauma), the expedition ends immediately and you have failed.

**Partial Success:**
You can reach the exit (Card 20) and escape, but your ending depends on Hope tokens and Passage marks collected (see Card 20: The Exit).

## 2. HOW TO PLAY (TURN BY TURN)

### The Basic Loop (Repeat for Each Card)

**Step 1: Reveal the Card**
1. The active player or GM flips the next Chapter card face-up.
2. Read the card's **title**, **flavor text**, and **card type** aloud.
3. Everyone takes 10 seconds to read the full card.
4. Discuss strategy for 30 seconds if needed.

**Step 2: Resolve the Card**
The card type determines what happens next:

#### If It's a **BASIC ENCOUNTER** or **EXPLORATION:**
- The card tells you to roll a test (e.g., "Force or Cunning vs d6").
- One player rolls (or all roll, depending on the card).
- Compare the result to the Dread Die.
- Apply success or failure consequences.

#### If It's a **HAZARD:**
- The card forces a group test (all Wayfarers roll, highest counts).
- Success or failure affects everyone.
- May create conditions that stick to the next room.

#### If It's a **CHOICE MOMENT:**
- The card offers 2-3 branches (A, B, C).
- The group votes on which path to take.
- Resolve that branch's test and consequences.

#### If It's a **BOSS** or **FINALE:**
- Special rules apply (see that card).
- These are the most dangerous rooms.

**Step 3: Apply Consequences**
- Add/remove Stress.
- Gain/lose Trauma.
- Gain/lose items from the loot pool.
- Mark any new conditions.

**Step 4: Move Forward**
- If you survive, move to the next Chapter card.
- If all Wayfarers have 6 Trauma, the expedition ends (loss).
- If you reach Card 20, proceed to the finale.

## 3. ROLLING TESTS

### Understanding a Test

Every test is formatted like this:

**"Force or Cunning vs d8"**

This means:
- One Wayfarer (or a group) rolls either their Force die OR Cunning die (their choice).
- Roll that die.
- Roll a d8 (the Dread Die).
- Compare the results.

### The Roll Resolution

**You roll your stat die. You get a number. The Dread Die rolls a number.**

**Success: Your roll ≥ Dread Die roll**
- You beat or tie the Dread Die.
- Apply the **Success** consequences.

**Failure: Your roll < Dread Die roll**
- You lose the roll.
- Apply the **Failure** consequences.
- (You still move forward; failure doesn't end the game.)

### Multiple Wayfarers Rolling

**"Highest Roll Counts" (on Hazards):**
- All Wayfarers roll their designated stat die.
- Compare the highest roll to the Dread Die.
- If the highest roll ≥ Dread Die, the group succeeds.

**"Lowest Roll Counts" (on some tests):**
- All Wayfarers roll their designated stat die.
- Compare the lowest roll to the Dread Die.
- If the lowest roll ≥ Dread Die, the group succeeds.
- If the lowest roll < Dread Die, the group fails.

**"Average Rolls" (on some ritual tests):**
- All Wayfarers roll.
- Add up the results and divide by the number of Wayfarers.
- Compare the average to the Dread Die.

### Your Dice

Each Wayfarer has 4 stat dice:

| Stat | Used For |
|------|----------|
| **Force** | Melee, physical strength, endurance |
| **Cunning** | Deception, stealth, clever solutions |
| **Resolve** | Willpower, courage, resisting fear |
| **Guard** | Defense, armor, blocking danger |

**Your starting dice are printed on your Wayfarer card.**

Example: Jax Meridian has Force d8, Cunning d6, Resolve d6, Guard d8.

### Modifying Your Roll

**Advantage Dice (Roll Extra):**
- Some cards or abilities let you "roll +1 die" or "step up your die."
- **Roll +1 die** means roll two dice of the same type and use the higher.
- **Step up** means upgrade: d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 → d20.

**Disadvantage (Roll Worse):**
- Some conditions penalize you: "-1 die step" means d8 → d6 → d4.
- Apply the penalty, then roll.

**Items That Modify Rolls:**
- Some items say "next Force test gets +1 die" or "+2 dice."
- These are one-time uses or passive bonuses.
- Add them to your roll before comparing to the Dread Die.

## 4. STRESS & TRAUMA

### What Is Stress?

**Stress** is temporary pressure. It drains but can recover.

- Each Wayfarer has a **Stress Limit** (printed on their card, usually 6-7).
- When you gain Stress, add it to your current total.
- Stress can be cleared by items, abilities, or success on certain cards.

### What Happens When You Hit Your Stress Limit?

**When your Stress reaches or exceeds your Stress Limit:**

1. **Do NOT add the Stress.**
2. **Instead, convert it to Trauma:**
   - Reduce your Stress to 0.
   - Gain 1 Trauma.

**Example:** Jax has a Stress Limit of 7. He's at 6 Stress. He gains 2 Stress.
- Normally 6 + 2 = 8, but 8 > 7.
- **Instead:** Jax's Stress resets to 0, and he gains 1 Trauma.

### What Is Trauma?

**Trauma** is permanent damage. It sticks with you.

- Each Wayfarer can have 0 to 6 Trauma.
- Some abilities can reduce Trauma (Brother Cal, some items).
- When you gain Trauma:
  - Add 1 to your Trauma counter.
  - Your stat dice may downgrade (see below).

### Trauma Effects

**For each Trauma you have, one of your stats degrades:**

| Trauma Level | Effect |
|--------------|--------|
| 0 Trauma | No penalty |
| 1 Trauma | Force d-1 step (Force becomes worse) |
| 2 Trauma | Force d-1 AND Cunning d-1 |
| 3 Trauma | Force d-1, Cunning d-1 AND Guard d-1 |
| 4 Trauma | All four stats d-1 |
| 5 Trauma | All four stats d-2 |
| 6 Trauma | **DEAD.** The expedition ends for you. |

**Example:** Ena starts with Cunning d10. She gains 2 Trauma.
- She now has Cunning d8 (stepped down twice).
- When she makes a Cunning test, she rolls d8 instead of d10.

## 5. ITEMS & EQUIPMENT

### What Are Items?

**Items** are one-time or passive bonuses you collect and use.

- Items are drawn from the loot pool on Chapter cards.
- When you successfully complete a card with loot, you draw items and keep some.
- Each Wayfarer can hold a limited number (usually 2-3, depends on card).

### Types of Items

**One-Time Items:**
- Use once, then discard.
- Example: "Song Cipher (one-time: reroll any test)"
- When you use it, mark it off and remove it from your inventory.

**Passive Items:**
- Always active. Don't "use" them.
- Example: "Bone Flute (passive: next Resolve test +1 die)"
- Apply the bonus when the situation triggers.

**Condition Items:**
- Remove a bad condition or status.
- Example: "Healing Salve (one-time: clear 1 Stress for each Wayfarer)"
- Use when you need to recover.

### Inventory Management

- You have a limited number of item slots (2-3 per Wayfarer).
- When you fill your slots, you must choose which items to keep.
- If you collect more items than slots, the rest go back to the loot pool.
- You can drop an item to pick up a new one.

### Item Tracking

**Keep a list on your Wayfarer sheet:**
```
Items Held:
- [Item 1] (Type: one-time / passive / condition)
- [Item 2] (Type: one-time / passive / condition)
- [Item 3] (Type: one-time / passive / condition)
```

## 6. ABILITIES & POWERS

### Every Wayfarer Has Three Abilities

Each Wayfarer card has three sections:

1. **Passive Ability** — Always active, always works.
2. **Once Per Room Ability** — Can use once per Chapter card.
3. **Break Ability** — Can use once per expedition, during desperate moments.

### How to Use Them

**Passive (Always On):**
- Example: Jax's "Hold Fast" lets ties count as successes when he leads Force/Guard tests.
- You don't "activate" it; just apply it when the situation happens.

**Once Per Room (Once Per Card):**
- Example: Ena's "Ghost Step" lets her reroll a failed Cunning or Resolve test.
- You may use this once per Chapter card.
- After you use it, mark it as "used this room."
- On the next Chapter card, you can use it again.

**Break (Once Per Expedition):**
- Example: Jax's "Not One More" saves another Wayfarer from Trauma.
- You can use this exactly once during the entire 20-card expedition.
- When you use it, mark it permanently so you remember you've spent it.
- If you reach Card 20 and haven't used it, it's wasted.

### Strategic Timing

- **Use Once Per Room wisely:** Save it for when you really need a reroll or boost.
- **Hold your Break:** Use it when a Wayfarer would die or when morale is critical.
- **Combine abilities:** Multiple Wayfarers can use abilities on the same test for stacking effects.

## 7. CONDITIONS & MARKERS

### What Are Conditions?

**Conditions** are temporary penalties or states that stick until a trigger clears them.

### Common Conditions

| Condition | Effect | Clears When |
|-----------|--------|------------|
| **Waterlogged** | Next test rolls at -1 die step | You lead 1 successful test |
| **Isolated** | Cannot help other Wayfarers | You lead 1 successful test |
| **Glowing** | Next test rolls at -1 die step | You lead 1 successful test |
| **Wounded** | Next test rolls at -1 die step | Any Wayfarer leads 1 successful test |
| **Disturbed** | Your next test rolls at -1 die step | You lead 1 successful test |
| **Buried** | Lose your next Once-Per-Room ability, gain 1 Trauma instead | You lead 1 successful test |
| **Marked** | Next hostile creature ignores your Guard abilities | Card effect states it clears |
| **Haunted** | Your next test rolls at -1 die step | You lead 1 successful test |
| **Answering** | Gain 1 Trauma at end of next room if you didn't lead a successful test | You lead 1 successful test |

### What Are Markers?

**Markers** are expedition-wide notes that affect future cards or the ending.

### Common Markers

| Marker | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Resonance** | At the next Failure, gain +1 Stress. Then erase. |
| **Echo** | If another Singing/Resolve test appears in next 4 cards, gain +1 Stress on failure. |
| **Haunted** | Mark in your journal. At end of expedition, if 2+ Haunted, final room changes. |
| **Pursued** | If second Hazard appears before end, it gets +1 Dread Die. |
| **Blessed** | At any future Failure by another Wayfarer, erase this to prevent 1 Stress. |
| **Harvest** | At any future Failure, erase this to reduce Stress by 1. |
| **Clarity** | At next Failure, erase this to prevent 1 Trauma. |
| **Hope Token** | Collect these. Spend at Card 20 for better endings. |
| **Victory** | Mark this when you beat the Bell Saint boss. Affects later narrative. |
| **Passage Granted** | Marked after beating the Final Vigil. Unlocks best endings. |
| **Owed** | Marked when you fail the Final Vigil. If 3+, expedition failed despite escape. |

## 8. WINNING & LOSING

### You Lose Immediately If:

- **All 4 Wayfarers reach 6 Trauma** — Everyone is dead. Expedition over.

### You Reach the Ending If:

- **You survive to Card 20** — Proceed to the finale.

### Card 20: The Exit (How You Actually Win)

**Final Test:**
- All Wayfarers choose a stat (Force, Cunning, Resolve, or Guard).
- Roll one die from each chosen stat.
- Average the rolls (round up).
- Compare to d12.

**On Success:**
You escape. Now check your ending tier:

| Hope Tokens | Passage Granted | Result |
|------------|-----------------|---------|
| 0-2 | No | **Bittersweet Exit** — You survived. Many did not. |
| 3-5 | No | **Triumphant Exit** — You will tell this story. |
| 6+ | Yes | **Legendary Exit** — You return changed, worthy, ready to go back. |
| Any | No, but 3+ Owed marks | **Pyrrhic Exit** — You escaped, but at what cost? |

**On Failure:**
- One final Wayfarer gains 1 Trauma.
- But the doors open anyway.
- **You stumble into light, exhausted.**
- This is not victory, but it is survival.

### Replay Value

The ending changes based on your choices throughout the expedition. Different players will collect different Hope tokens and Passage marks, leading to different epilogues on replays.

## 9. QUICK REFERENCE

### Core Mechanic (Repeat Every Card)

```
1. Reveal card
2. Read aloud
3. Discuss strategy (30 sec)
4. Roll test (if needed)
5. Compare to Dread Die
6. Apply consequences (Stress/Trauma/items)
7. Move to next card
```

### Rolling a Test

```
Player rolls [Stat Die] vs GM rolls [Dread Die]
If Player ≥ Dread Die → Success
If Player < Dread Die → Failure
```

### When You Get Stressed

```
Current Stress + New Stress = Total
If Total > Stress Limit:
  → Set Stress to 0
  → Gain 1 Trauma
Otherwise:
  → Add the Stress normally
```

### Stat Die Degradation (from Trauma)

```
Starting: Force d8, Cunning d6, Resolve d6, Guard d8

1 Trauma:  Force d6 (Force only)
2 Trauma:  Force d6, Cunning d4 (Force + Cunning)
3 Trauma:  Force d6, Cunning d4, Guard d6 (three stats)
4 Trauma:  All stats d-1 step
5 Trauma:  All stats d-2 steps
6 Trauma:  DEAD
```

### Item Flow

```
Encounter offers loot
→ Draw X items
→ Keep Y items
→ Rest go back to pool or discard
→ Track what you're holding
→ Use one-time items when needed
```

### Ability Flow

```
Passive:        Always on. Apply automatically.
Once Per Room:  Use once per card. Resets each new card.
Break:          Use once per entire expedition. Mark when used.
```

### Hope Tokens

```
Gain from certain cards (usually Choice Moments or victories)
Spend at Card 20 to improve your ending
Track how many you have
```

## TEACHING THE GAME (5-Minute Version)

### Tell New Players This:

**"This is a doomed-adventure card game. You're trying to survive 20 rooms.**

**Here's how:**

1. **Every room is a card.** We reveal it and read it aloud.

2. **Most rooms have a test.** You roll your stat die (Force, Cunning, Resolve, or Guard). We roll a Dread Die. If you're equal or higher, you win. Otherwise, you lose—but you still move forward. Failure doesn't kill you; Trauma does.

3. **Stress is temporary.** You can gain Stress up to your limit (usually 7). But if you hit the limit, you convert it to Trauma instead.

4. **Trauma is permanent.** Each Trauma makes one of your stats worse. At 6 Trauma, you're dead.

5. **You have abilities.** A Passive (always works), a Once-Per-Room (use it when you need a reroll or boost), and a Break ability (use once the entire game when things are desperate).

6. **Items are relief.** When you win a room with loot, you draw items. One-time items reroll tests or clear Stress. Passive items give bonuses.

7. **You win if you reach Card 20 and pass the final test.** Your ending depends on Hope tokens and Passage marks. Better choices = better ending.

8. **You lose if all four of you hit 6 Trauma.**

**That's it. Let's play.**"

## FAQ (Frequently Asked Questions)

### "Can I use my Once-Per-Room ability on every card?"

Yes. Each card resets it. So you can use it on Card 1, then again on Card 2, but only once per card.

### "What if I'm at Stress limit and gain 2 Stress? Do I get 2 Trauma?"

No. You only convert once. Stress resets to 0, you gain 1 Trauma. The extra Stress is lost (it doesn't overflow).

### "Can I help other Wayfarers on their rolls?"

Sometimes. Cards will say "all Wayfarers roll" or "group test." On those, everyone participates. On solo tests, only that Wayfarer rolls.

### "Can I spend a Hope token to avoid Trauma?"

No. Hope tokens are only spent at Card 20 to affect your ending. Items and abilities can prevent Trauma; Hope cannot.

### "What if we fail at the Final Vigil boss?"

You still escape Card 20 with the "Pyrrhic Exit" unless you have too many "Owed" marks (3+). Then the expedition failed even though you left alive.

### "Can I use my Break ability to save myself?"

Depends on the ability. Most Break abilities save other Wayfarers. Read your card carefully. Some Break abilities save you, some save others, some do both.

### "If I'm Buried (from the Stair Descends card), what happens?"

You lose your Once-Per-Room ability for the next room and gain 1 Trauma instead. After you lead a successful test, you're freed and can use it again.

### "Do conditions stack?"

Yes. You can have multiple conditions at once. Each one applies its penalty independently.

### "How long does a full game take?"

About 90–120 minutes with 4 players. Faster with fewer players or if you skip card descriptions.

### "Can we shuffle the deck or play cards out of order?"

The 20-card deck is in a specific order for pacing. First playtest: play in order. After that, feel free to shuffle or customize.

### "What if someone dies mid-game?"

If a Wayfarer reaches 6 Trauma, they're dead. Flip their card over. The other Wayfarers continue. If all four die, the expedition ends immediately (loss).
