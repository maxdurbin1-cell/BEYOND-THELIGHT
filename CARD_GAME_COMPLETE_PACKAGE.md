## RECOMMENDED CANVA WORKFLOW

### Step 1: Design Wayfarer Cards (1 hour)
- **Template:** Large card (3x5" landscape)
- **Sections:** Name | Title | Flavor line | Stat dice (grid) | Stress Limit | Item Slots | Passive (box) | Once Per Room (highlighted box) | Break (dark footer box)
- **Visual:** One bold portrait per character, thematic color per Wayfarer
- **Color Scheme:** Jax = Iron/grey, Ena = Purple/void, Brother Cal = Dust/tan, Mira = Salvage/rust

### Step 2: Design Chapter Cards (2–3 hours)
- **Template:** Medium card (3x5" landscape)
- **Sections:** Title | Flavor text (italics, small) | Card Type (badge) | Dread Die (prominent) | Test description | Success/Failure boxes | Loot list (if any) | Markers/Tags (small footer)
- **Color Scheme:** Threshold (cool), Pressure (warm), Climax (hot), Endgame (ominous)
- **Visual:** Atmospheric illustration or abstract texture per card

### Step 3: Design Mutation Cards (1 hour)
- **Template:** Small card (2.5x3.5" or 3x3")
- **Sections:** Mutation name | Visual description (1 line) | Mechanics (bullet points) | Roleplay (bullet points)
- **Color Scheme:** Dark, unsettling; all mutations should feel alien and permanent
- **Visual:** Symbolic or horrific illustration per mutation

### Step 4: Design Personal Flavor Cards (45 min)
- **Template:** Small card (2.5x3.5")
- **Sections:** Character name | Flavor title | Tagline (italics) | When to Use | Effect (clear bullet points)
- **Color Scheme:** Match character color from Wayfarer card
- **Visual:** Thematic moment (protection, prescience, healing, salvage)

### Step 5: Design Monster Cards (45 min, optional)
- **Template:** Large card (5x7" or poster size, or 3x5" with two-sided print)
- **Sections:** Name | Health | Dread Die | Special Trait | Abilities (listed) | Actions (table) | Loot
- **Color Scheme:** Ominous, iconic; each monster should feel unique
- **Visual:** Striking illustration of the creature/presence

## PRINTING SPECIFICATIONS

### Card Stock
- **Wayfarer & Chapter Cards:** 110 lb cardstock (3x5" landscape)
- **Mutation & Flavor Cards:** 100 lb cardstock (2.5x3.5" or 3x3")
- **Monster Cards:** 110 lb cardstock (5x7" or larger)

### Finishes
- **Matte:** Better for readability, easier to write on (tracking sheets)
- **Glossy:** More durable, professional look, harder to read in glare

### Local Print Shops vs Online
- **Local (FedEx, UPS, local print shop):** Faster, can see proofs, good for small runs (1–5 decks)
- **Online (MakePlayingCards, DriveThruCards):** Cheaper for larger runs (10+ decks), slower shipping

## FIRST PLAYTEST CHECKLIST

### Before Playing:
- [ ] Print all cards
- [ ] Organize into decks
- [ ] Gather 4 d4, 4 d6, 4 d8, 4 d10, 4 d12 (2 sets of standard dice)
- [ ] Print Rules Reference (1 per player + 1 for GM)
- [ ] Print Cheat Sheet (optional but helpful)
- [ ] Have paper and pencil for tracking Stress/Trauma

### Setup (5 min):
- [ ] Each player picks a Wayfarer card
- [ ] Each player draws a Mutation card
- [ ] Each player receives their 2 Personal Flavor cards
- [ ] Create a tracking sheet (see Rules Reference, section 9)

### Playing:
- [ ] Start with Chapter Card 1
- [ ] Follow the core loop: Reveal → Test → Resolve → Next Card
- [ ] Use simple test mode for bosses (Cards 11, 19) on first playtest
- [ ] Optional: Use Monster cards on second playtest

### After Playing:
- [ ] Did the pacing feel right? (Should be 90–120 min)
- [ ] Did anyone use all their abilities/flavors? (Should be reserved/strategic)
- [ ] Did mutations feel impactful? (Should alter at least one test or roleplay moment)
- [ ] Did the ending feel earned? (Should feel like your choices mattered)
