## STEP 1 - RAID EXPEDITIONS OF THE COLLAPSING PROVINCE

### I. Archivist Preface: What a Raid Is

A Raid Expedition is not a dungeon crawl. It is a military pilgrimage through a living province that remembers old catastrophes.

Players are not only trying to survive combat. They are managing collapse pressure, choosing which portals to close, and deciding whether to spend strength now or save it for the Raid Lord.

In plain terms: a Raid is a 3-stage campaign run.

- Day 1 Boss: d8 Dread, 16 HP.
- Day 2 Boss: d10 Dread, 20 HP.
- Day 3 Raid Boss: d20 Dread, 40 HP.

If the party closes enough portals before Day 3, the Raid Boss is weakened.

- Portal objective: close 5 portals.
- If completed: Raid Boss is reduced to d12 Dread, 24 HP.

This is the central strategic promise of the Raid system: exploration and puzzle success meaningfully alter the end boss fight.

---

### II. Raid Setup Procedure (GM Reference)

When opening a Raid Expedition, do the following in order.

1. Generate the province map and stamp features.
2. Set expedition day to 1.
3. Seed mini-boss and field threat spaces.
4. Set flask values (3 current, 7 max).
5. Set portal objective tracker (0/5).
6. Set gate tracker (0 sealed).
7. Determine Raid Boss identity from the Raid Lord pool.

Raid Lord pool includes:

- Azrael
- Mephisto
- The Hollow Saint
- The Bone Regent
- The Blackened Throne
- The Rift Shepherd

Recommended table script:

"Choose your armor, your weapon, and your Personal Flavor. The Province is already moving against you."

---

### III. Encounter Ladder and Threat Roles

Use the following threat ladder when pacing combat in the expedition.

- Field Enemy: d4 Dread, 4 HP.
- Mini Boss: d6 Dread, 12 HP.
- Day 1 Boss: d8 Dread, 16 HP.
- Day 2 Boss: d10 Dread, 20 HP.
- Raid Boss: d20 Dread, 40 HP.

Design note for print: keep this ladder in a sidebar on every Raid spread.

Raid flavor profiles should be described by role at table:

- Field threats: ambushers and attrition hunters.
- Mini bosses: pressure spikes that can tax party resources.
- Day bosses: phase gatekeepers.
- Raid boss: final threat identity and narrative culmination.

---

### IV. Portal and Gate Operations

#### A. Closing Gates

When a player stands on an active gate hex, they may take the Close Gate action.

On success:

- Mark gate as sealed.
- Increase Gates Closed by 1.
- Trigger immediate transit jump (relocate to a random open hex).

Narrative guidance:

A gate seal is tactical repositioning disguised as ritual action. Encourage players to use it for both progress and board-state advantage.

#### B. Portal Breach Combat

If players breach an active portal:

- Spawn 1-4 portal guards.
- Typical portal guards operate at d4 Dread, 4 HP.
- Combat must be cleared before the portal puzzle can begin.

#### C. Portal Seal Puzzle

After portal combat, run a puzzle resolution.

- Preferred mode: pipe-flow style conduit lock.
- Fallback mode: Mind vs DD8 check.

On puzzle success:

- Portal is closed.
- Portals Closed tracker increases.
- Check Raid Boss nerf threshold (5+ closed).

On puzzle failure:

- Portal remains dangerous.
- Collapse pressure advances (edge loss/backlash event).

GM tone prompt:

"The breach does not merely open. It learns."

### V. Raid Loot System (Print-Ready)

Raid loot is intentionally split between tactical tools and build-defining trophies.

#### A. Boss-Class Loot Rules

Defeating field bosses, mini bosses, day bosses, and raid bosses awards equipment-biased loot with affixes.

- Primary categories: weapons, armor.
- Boss loot receives a random affix tag.

Affix pool (current):

- Ashbound
- Moonchained
- Thornwake
- Hollowglass
- Dreadforged
- Graven
- Saltfire
- Umbral

#### B. Utility Loot Rules

Consumable and utility categories can roll bonus potency.

- Eligible categories include scroll/item/mod-style drops.
- 35% chance to gain bonus tag: AD+1.

#### C. Example Book Formatting for Loot Entries

Use this style in print:

- Stormpike Halberd [Dreadforged]
- Pilgrim Plate [Moonchained]
- Null Vial [AD+1]
- Rationed Elixir [AD+1]

#### D. Run Loot Cache Rule

Expedition loot remains run-scoped unless explicitly transferred by your campaign economy procedure.

Design intent:

Raid runs should feel like a pressured rogue operation with meaningful but temporary loadout decisions.

---

### VI. Boss Defeat Rewards and Legacy Tracking

When a Raid Boss is defeated:

- Record expedition clear.
- Award +3 Raid Points.
- Mark run as successful in legacy tracking.

Use this to feed later systems (endgame contracts, title unlocks, and narrative standing).

---

### VII. GM Pacing: The Raid in Three Acts

Run every Raid using three dramatic acts.

Act I - Recon and Extraction Pressure

- Teach map pressure quickly.
- Offer one low-risk cache win.
- Introduce at least one field ambush.

Act II - Attrition and Strategic Choices

- Increase mini-boss frequency.
- Force portal puzzle timing decisions.
- Make the party choose between healing routes and objective routes.

Act III - Lordfall

- Reveal whether the portal objective changed the Raid Lord.
- Run a cinematic arrival line for the Raid Boss.
- Close with a visible legacy reward (Raid Points, named trophy, or map state shift).

---

### VIII. Ready-to-Print Rules Summary (Player Handout)

Raid Expedition Quick Rules

- Complete Day 1, Day 2, and Day 3 boss path.
- Close portals whenever possible. At 5 closed portals, the Raid Boss is weakened.
- Use gates for seal progress and forced repositioning.
- Expect boss ladder escalation: d8/16 -> d10/20 -> d20/40.
- Boss trophies favor weapons/armor and carry affixes.
- Defeat the Raid Boss to gain +3 Raid Points.
