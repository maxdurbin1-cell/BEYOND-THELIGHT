# The Known Realm of Theos: Codex Lore

## Overview
Theos is a world forged in the crucible of apocalypse and rebirth. Once part of the fabled World That Was, Theos now stands as a testament to survival, mutation, and the relentless pursuit of hope beneath a dying sun. Its history is a tapestry of shattered epochs, strange gifts, and the ever-present shadow of cosmic exile.

---

## The Great Sundering and the World That Was
Long ago, the earth was bathed in a storm of radiant fire. The World That Was—an age of wonders, technology, and hubris—collapsed under the weight of its own ambition. The Sundering irradiated the land, warping flesh and spirit alike. Some survivors gained Gifts: unique powers, mutations, or curses, each a personal echo of the cataclysm.

In desperation, the architects of the World That Was raised the Barrier: a shimmering veil that sealed away the heart of the old world. The chosen were sent beyond, cast adrift among the Stars in search of a new home. Those left behind—by fate or design—became the first denizens of Theos.

---

## Theos: Provinces and Peoples
Theos is divided into ancient Provinces, each a realm of mutated survivors, new societies, and forgotten technologies. Over generations, these peoples have forged new cultures atop the bones of the old, their myths and customs shaped by the Gifts and the ever-present memory of exile.

- **The Starward Marches:** Borderlands haunted by memories of the exodus, where sky-worshippers and scavenger-tribes vie for relics.
- **The Iron Valleys:** Industrial ruins and fungal forests, ruled by guilds of machinists and fleshcrafters.
- **The Luminous Steppe:** A land of radiant grasses and nomadic Gifted, where mutation is celebrated as divine.
- **The Shrouded Bastions:** Fortress-cities built around ancient reactors, home to secretive orders and scholars of the Barrier.

---

## The Gifts and the Mutated
Every child of Theos is marked by the Sundering. Some bear visible mutations—crimson eyes, crystalline skin, spectral limbs—while others wield subtler Gifts: the power to heal, to command machines, to glimpse echoes of the past. These abilities are both blessing and curse, shaping destinies and fueling the ambitions of would-be rulers, prophets, and outcasts.

---

## The Death of the Old Sun
Theos orbits a dying star. Prophets and astronomers alike warn of the coming end: one hundred days until the Old Sun’s final eclipse. Legends speak of the New Sun—a cosmic rebirth that may yet save the world, if the right rituals, sacrifices, or technologies can be found. The race is on: factions, cults, and lone wanderers seek the means to ignite the dawn.

---

## Factions and Mysteries (Hooks for Expansion)
- **The Solar Cults:** Competing faiths who claim to know the secret of the New Sun.
- **The Exiles’ Lineage:** Descendants of those sent to the Stars, rumored to return with alien knowledge.
- **The Barrier Wardens:** Guardians of the last gates into the World That Was.
- **The Bone Oracles:** Mutated seers who read the future in the marrow of the dead.
- **The Crucible Guilds:** Machinists and alchemists who seek to master mutation itself.

---

## Timeline of Theos
1. **The World That Was:** Age of wonders, hubris, and the rise of the Barrier.
2. **The Sundering:** Cataclysmic irradiation, emergence of Gifts, collapse of old order.
3. **The Exile:** Chosen sent to the Stars, the Barrier sealed.
4. **The Age of Mutation:** New societies form, Gifts become central to culture and power.
5. **The Dimming:** The Old Sun fades, prophecies of the New Sun spread.
6. **The Final Days:** One hundred days remain; the fate of Theos hangs in the balance.

## Myths and Legends
- **The Starborn Return:** One day, the exiles will return, bearing the secret of the New Sun.
- **The Sleeping Machine:** Deep beneath the Iron Valleys, a machine dreams of restoring the world.
- **The Gifted King:** A ruler will arise, uniting the Provinces and leading Theos into the dawn.
