# Card Expedition: Monster Cards (2 Cards)

## What Are Monster Cards?

**Monster Cards** replace simple Dread tests with full boss encounters.

- Use these instead of the single-test version on Cards 11 and 19.
- Each monster has multiple rounds, special abilities, and escalating pressure.
- Monsters can be **wounded** and may **reappear** if the expedition continues beyond Card 19.
- These are the "cinematic moments" where combat feels like a real scene, not just a die roll.

**Print these 2 cards. Use them in place of the single Dread test on Cards 11 and 19. Monsters are fully optional—if you want fast play, stick with single tests.**

---

## MONSTER CARD 1: THE BELL SAINT

**Used For:** Card 11 (Climax, First Boss)

---

### IDENTITY

**Visual:** A figure of oxidized bronze and cracked bell-glass, impossibly tall. It has no face, only a seam where sound leaks out. Every step leaves ringing air.

**Dread Die:** d12  
**Health:** 10 stress  
**Special Trait:** **Resonant** — Every round, it grows louder. Its Dread Die increases by +1 each round (d12 → becomes harder to oppose).

---

### ROUND STRUCTURE

**Each "round" of combat consists of:**

1. **The Bell Saint's Turn (Resolve vs d12):**
   - All Wayfarers roll Resolve or Guard together (highest roll counts).
   - Bell Saint attacks with its presence.
   
2. **Wayfarers' Turns (Actions, up to 2 per Wayfarer):**
   - Each Wayfarer may take one action: Strike, Defend, or Inspire.

3. **Resolve Phase:**
   - Apply damage and consequences.

---

### THE BELL SAINT'S ABILITIES

#### Start of Round: The Bells Ring

**Automatic.** All Wayfarers gain 1 Stress (the sound is relentless).

On **Round 3+**, increase this to 2 Stress (the noise gets unbearable).

---

#### Attack: Toll of Regret

**All Wayfarers roll together: Resolve or Guard vs d12.**

**On Success (Beat or Tie):**
- The Wayfarers brace against the sound.
- No additional damage this round.

**On Failure:**
- Each Wayfarer gains 2 Stress.
- One Wayfarer (choice) gains 1 Trauma (the sound breaks something inside).

---

#### Special Phase (After Round 2): Crescendo

**If the Bell Saint is still alive after Round 2:**

On its next attack, its Dread Die **increases by +2** (instead of +1).

This represents the noise reaching an unbearable peak.

---

### WAYFARER ACTIONS (During Each Wayfarer's Turn)

Each Wayfarer can take **one action per round.**

#### Action 1: Strike/Assault (Force vs d12)

**Attack the Bell Saint directly.**

- Roll Force.
- Compare to d12 (Bell Saint's current Dread Die).

**On Success:**
- Damage the Bell Saint: reduce its health by 1 stress.
- Narrate breaking part of it (cracking bell-glass, denting bronze, silencing a pipe).

**On Failure:**
- The attack rebounds. That Wayfarer gains 2 Stress (the sound explodes outward).

---

#### Action 2: Defend (Guard vs d8)

**Rally the Wayfarers against the sound.**

- One Wayfarer rolls Guard vs d8.
- This is a group defense action.

**On Success:**
- All Wayfarers clear 1 Stress (you brace together).
- The Bell Saint's next attack gets -1 die (you created a momentary barrier).

**On Failure:**
- The Wayfarers scatter. Next round, the Bell Saint's attack gets +1 die.

---

#### Action 3: Inspire (Resolve vs d10)

**Bolster a Wayfarer's will.**

- One Wayfarer rolls Resolve.

**On Success:**
- Choose one Wayfarer (can be yourself). That Wayfarer clears 2 Stress OR gains +1 die on their next test this combat.
- The Bell Saint perceives this defiance and is briefly distracted.

**On Failure:**
- Your inspiration falters. The Wayfarer you tried to inspire gains 1 Stress instead.

---

#### Action 4: Use an Item (No roll)

**Spend a one-time item to:**
- Clear Stress
- Gain +1 die on next roll
- Prevent 1 Trauma

Items can be used out of turn to react to consequences.

---

### DAMAGE & DEFEAT

**The Bell Saint is Defeated When:**
- Its health reaches 0 stress (Wayfarers have damaged it 10 times total across the round).

**On Defeat:**
- The Bell Saint bows and recedes into shadow.
- All Wayfarers heal all Stress immediately.
- Gain 2 rare items from the loot pool.
- One Wayfarer recovers 1 Trauma (choose who).
- Mark **Victory** in your journal.

---

### PARTIAL SUCCESS / RETREAT

**If Wayfarers want to disengage or escape after Round 2+:**

Each Wayfarer makes a **Guard or Cunning test vs d10**.

**On Success:**
- You break free. Move to the next Chapter card.
- You escape with no additional Trauma, but the Bell Saint is marked as **Wounded but Awake**.
- If you face 3+ more rooms, the Bell Saint **may reappear** (GM's choice or shuffle it into remaining cards as a 2-in-6 chance).

**On Failure:**
- You can't escape. Forced to continue combat.

---

### BELL SAINT LOOT (Defeated)

When the Bell Saint is defeated, draw from this pool:

- **Saint Bell Relic** (passive: one Wayfarer gains +1 die to Resolve until expedition ends)
- **Chiming Vial** (one-time: all Wayfarers clear 2 Stress)
- **Muted Shard** (one-time: next Resolve test gets +2 dice)
- **Bells of Passage** (one-time: skip the next Chapter card, move directly ahead)

---

### THE BELL SAINT WOUNDED STATE

If the Bell Saint survives and Wayfarers escape:

**It's Marked: Wounded but Awake**

If it reappears later (Card 19 or shuffle back):

- Its Health starts at 5 stress (halfway) instead of 10.
- Its opening Dread Die is d10 (reduced from d12).
- But it's **angry**—it gains +1 Stress to its attacks.

## MONSTER CARD 2: THE FINAL VIGIL

**Used For:** Card 19 (Endgame, Second Boss / Ritual Encounter)

---

### IDENTITY

**Visual:** A figure stands at the expedition's threshold. It is made of everything you've lost here. It is every room that hurt. It is the shape of your regrets. It has no eyes—only the echo of eyes. It asks: "Will you leave anyone behind?"

**Dread Die:** d12  
**Health:** 15 stress  
**Special Trait:** **Parasitic** — The more it damages Wayfarers, the stronger it becomes. Each Wayfarer's Trauma makes it harder to defeat.

---

### ROUND STRUCTURE

**Each "round" of ritual combat:**

1. **The Vigil's Whisper (Resolve vs d12):**
   - All Wayfarers roll Resolve together (lowest roll counts—this is about mutual will, not individual strength).

2. **Wayfarers' Collective Turn:**
   - All Wayfarers must act **together** on this turn.
   - No individual actions. You are united or you are fallen.

3. **Resolve Phase:**
   - Apply damage and existential consequences.

---

### THE VIGIL'S ABILITIES

#### Start of Round: Whispers of Regret

**Automatic.** Each Wayfarer must describe one regret from this expedition aloud (something they're leaving behind, something they failed to save, a choice that haunts them).

After describing, each Wayfarer gains 1 Stress (the weight of memory).

---

#### Attack: Reach for One

**All Wayfarers roll together: lowest Resolve roll vs d12.**

**Compare Lowest Roll to d12:**

**On Success (Lowest Roll ≥ d12):**
- Your collective will is unbroken.
- The Vigil recoils. No damage this round.

**On Failure (Lowest Roll < d12):**
- The Vigil reaches out and grasps one Wayfarer (the one with the lowest roll, or group chooses).
- That Wayfarer gains 2 Stress.
- You must choose: Does that Wayfarer gain 1 Trauma? Or do all other Wayfarers gain 1 Stress to save them?

---

#### Special Phase (After Round 2): Manifestation

**If the Vigil is still present after Round 2:**

It fully manifests. The next attack is more potent:

- Its Dread Die increases by +2 (from d12 to d14-equivalent, or re-roll twice and take higher).
- All Wayfarers gain an additional 1 Stress from the manifestation pressure.

---

### WAYFARER ACTIONS (Collective Turn)

All Wayfarers must act **together**. Choose one collective action:

#### Action 1: Stand United (Resolve, Group Roll)

**All Wayfarers roll Resolve together. Sum the highest 2 rolls.**

**Compare Sum to d12:**

**On Success:**
- Your unity is strength. You inflict 1 damage on the Vigil (reduce health by 1).
- All Wayfarers clear 1 Stress (you supported each other).

**On Failure:**
- Your bond fractures momentarily. Each Wayfarer gains 2 Stress.

---

#### Action 2: Sacrifice (Ritual Test)

**One Wayfarer volunteers to carry the group's burden.**

That Wayfarer makes a **Resolve test vs d12 alone** (not grouped).

**On Success:**
- That Wayfarer gains 1 Trauma.
- But the Vigil is wounded: reduce its health by 2 stress.
- All other Wayfarers clear all Stress (the sacrifice carries their weight).

**On Failure:**
- The Vigil consumes the sacrifice. That Wayfarer gains 2 Trauma immediately.
- The Vigil heals 1 stress (it fed).

---

#### Action 3: Demand Answers (Cunning, Group Roll)

**All Wayfarers roll Cunning together. Average the rolls (round up).**

**Compare Average to d12:**

**On Success:**
- The Vigil must answer one question truthfully (GM answers based on the Chapter deck and expedition narrative).
- This is not combat damage, but **knowledge damage**—it reveals something that helps you next round.
- Gain +1 die on your next collective roll.

**On Failure:**
- The Vigil lies. You gain false knowledge (GM describes a tempting but wrong answer).
- Next round, your rolls are at -1 die (distracted by the lie).

---

#### Action 4: Spend Hope (Resource Action)

**Any Wayfarer may spend a Hope token (max 1 per round).**

- Gain +1 die on this round's collective roll.
- Spend as many Hope tokens as you want (each +1).

(This is the only way to "cheat" against the Vigil—by spending the rewards you earned from earlier choices.)

---

### DAMAGE & DEFEAT

**The Vigil is Defeated When:**
- Its health reaches 0 stress (Wayfarers have inflicted 15 damage across rounds).

**On Defeat:**
- The figure bows. It releases what it holds.
- All Wayfarers heal all Stress immediately.
- Each Wayfarer recovers 1 Trauma (choose which Trauma slot to heal for each).
- Gain 3 rare items total from the loot pool (shared).
- Mark **Passage Granted** in your journal.
- Proceed directly to Card 20 (The Exit). You've earned the best possible ending.

---

### PARTIAL DEFEAT / SURVIVAL

**If Wayfarers want to disengage after Round 2+:**

Make a **group Resolve test: average all Resolve dice vs d12.**

**On Success:**
- You break free spiritually. The Vigil releases you.
- Proceed to Card 20.
- Mark **Survived** (no Passage Granted).
- Your ending will be Bittersweet or Triumphant depending on Hope tokens.

**On Failure:**
- The Vigil holds you. Must continue or fall.
- Forced to continue combat next round.

---

### VIGIL LOOT (Defeated)

When the Final Vigil is defeated, gain these items:

- **Final Vigil Torch** (passive: one Wayfarer gains +1 die to Resolve until expedition ends)
- **Passage Seal** (one-time: skip the next Chapter card, if you were heading into optional rooms)
- **Regret Mirror** (one-time: all Wayfarers clear 3 Stress)
- **Vow Stone** (one-time: at any future Failure, prevent 2 Stress)

---

### VIGIL ESCALATION RULES

**The Vigil gets harder as Wayfarers gain Trauma:**

For each Wayfarer that has 3+ Trauma at the start of combat:
- Add +1 to the Vigil's Dread Die (per Wayfarer).

**Example:** If two Wayfarers have 3+ Trauma, the Vigil's d12 becomes d14 (re-roll twice, take higher).

This represents the Vigil feeding on broken will.

---

### THEMATIC NOTES

**The Final Vigil is not a battle you "win" in the traditional sense.**

You can:
1. **Defeat it cleanly** (with good Resolve and Hope tokens) = Legendary ending.
2. **Survive it** (disengage and escape) = Triumphant ending.
3. **Fall to it** (forced to continue, lose more Trauma) = Pyrrhic ending.

**The Vigil is a manifestation of your collective guilt.** The only way to truly "beat" it is to accept what you've lost and move forward anyway.

## Monster Card Combat Summary

### The Bell Saint (Card 11)
- **Threat Level:** High (10 health)
- **Combat Style:** Escalating noise, individual attacks
- **Best Stat:** Resolve or Guard
- **Escape:** Possible at Round 2+ (Guard/Cunning test vs d10)
- **Return:** May reappear if badly wounded

### The Final Vigil (Card 19)
- **Threat Level:** Extreme (15 health)
- **Combat Style:** Collective ritual, guilt-based
- **Best Stat:** Resolve (group)
- **Escape:** Possible at Round 2+ (group Resolve test vs d12)
- **Return:** Cannot return (final boss)

---

## Using Monsters in Your First Playtest

**Option 1: Simple (Recommended for first play)**
- Play Cards 11 and 19 as single Dread tests (from the Chapter deck).
- Use Monster cards on your second playtest when you're comfortable.

**Option 2: Full Combat**
- Replace Cards 11 and 19 tests with these Monster cards.
- Expect those two rounds to take 15–20 minutes each (instead of 2–3 min for Dread tests).
- Much more cinematic. Much more dramatic.

**Pick what feels right for your table.**
