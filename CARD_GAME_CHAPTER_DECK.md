# Card Expedition: Chapter Deck (20 Cards)

## Deck Structure & Pacing

| Band | Cards | Threat | Role | Dread Dice |
|------|-------|--------|------|-----------|
| **Threshold** | 1–4 | Warm up, learn the rooms | Establish tone | d6–d8 |
| **Pressure** | 5–9 | Climbing stakes, first elite | Tension rises | d8–d10 |
| **Climax** | 10–16 | Full danger, first boss, scars mount | Desperate | d10–d12 |
| **Endgame** | 17–20 | Final gauntlet, second boss or nemesis | Break or escape | d12 |

---

## BAND 1: THRESHOLD (Cards 1–4)
**Goal:** Players learn the room structure, get comfortable with one roll per card, feel the vibe.

### Card 1: Bone Gate
**Type:** BASIC ENCOUNTER  
**Dread Die:** d6

*A skeletal archway marks the deep places. Bone-white and humming. Your guide died here once before, they say.*

**Test:** Force or Cunning vs d6  
Succeed if you beat or tie.

**On Success:**
You pass through without disturbance. Keep 1 item from the reward pool or clear 1 Stress.

**On Failure:**
The gate protests. Each Wayfarer gains 1 Stress. Continue to the next room.

**Loot:** Basic item pool (Rope, Lamp, Medical Kit)

---

### Card 2: Flooded Chapel
**Type:** BASIC ENCOUNTER  
**Dread Die:** d6

*Water rises to the vaulted ceiling. Pews are half-submerged. Something moves below the surface, but it's not hostile—just old.*

**Test:** Cunning or Resolve vs d6

**On Success:**
You navigate safely. Gain 1 item from the reward pool. Mark 1 Stress on the room as "flooded knowledge" — on the next Hazard, you may erase this mark to reduce Stress gained by 1.

**On Failure:**
The water is colder than expected. All Wayfarers gain 1 Stress. If any Wayfarer now has 2+ Stress, they also gain the **Waterlogged** condition (next test rolls at -1 die step). Condition clears after 1 successful test.

**Loot:** Damp Scrolls, Waterproof Satchel, Salt Vial

---

### Card 3: Rust Garden
**Type:** EXPLORATION  
**Dread Die:** d6

*Metal flowers, corroded and humming. Some are still warm. The air tastes like copper and regret.*

**Test:** Cunning or Force vs d6

**On Success:**
Beautiful salvage. Draw 2 items and keep 1. Or: Choose one Wayfarer. That Wayfarer may step up one stat die until the end of the next room.

**On Failure:**
The flowers turn sharp. Each Wayfarer gains 1 Stress. Gain 1 item (your choice) as compensation—the garden's apology.

**Loot:** Rust Revolver, Copper Coil, Shattered Lens, Corroded Key

---

### Card 4: The Listening Well
**Type:** CHOICE MOMENT  
**Dread Die:** d6

*A shaft descends into warm dark. You can hear something breathing far below. It's not angry. It's waiting.*

**No Test Required.** Choose one:

**A) Call Down (Resolve vs d6)**
The thing below answers. On success: gain a cryptic rumor — mark it. At any future Failure, erase the rumor to add +1 to one roll. On failure: gain 1 Stress and all Wayfarers are **Marked** (next hostile creature ignores Guard abilities).

**B) Leave It Alone (No roll)**
Gain 1 item (Rope, Candle, Salt Charm). You move past cleanly but your guide looks afraid.

**C) Send Someone Down (Guard test vs d6)**
Risky. On success: gain 2 items of your choice and 1 Trauma recovers. On failure: that Wayfarer gains 2 Stress and is **Isolated** until they lead the next successful test.

---

## BAND 2: PRESSURE (Cards 5–9)
**Goal:** First real consequences. One elite encounter. Stress climbs. Trauma starts appearing.

### Card 5: Bone Elevator
**Type:** HAZARD  
**Dread Die:** d8

*Bone-scaffold and rope. It creaks as you rise. Below, the floor recedes. The cables fray as you watch.*

**Test:** Guard vs d8  
All Wayfarers roll together (highest roll counts). If tied or beaten, one Wayfarer gains 1 Stress.

**On Success:**
You reach the platform. Gain 1 item or clear 2 Stress collectively (split how you like).

**On Failure:**
A cable snaps. Each Wayfarer gains 2 Stress. One Wayfarer gains 1 Trauma (choose who). Move to the next card.

**Hazard Tag:** **Trapped** — if another Hazard appears in the next 3 cards, its Dread Die increases by one step.

---

### Card 6: The Organ Choir
**Type:** ELITE ENCOUNTER  
**Dread Die:** d10  
**Special:** This is your first real pressure moment.

*Bone pipes, each the size of a wayfarer, arranged in a choir. When the wind blows, they sing. The singing is not accidental.*

**Test:** Resolve vs d10

**On Success:**
The song dies. Gain 2 items or 1 rare item. Choose one Wayfarer: that Wayfarer clears all Stress.

**On Failure:**
The song gets louder. Each Wayfarer gains 2 Stress. One Wayfarer gains 1 Trauma (your choice). The room's presence lingers: mark **Resonance**. At the next Failure, Resonance activates and that Wayfarer gains an additional 1 Stress. Then erase Resonance.

**Loot:** Bone Flute (passive: next Resolve test +1 die), Song Cipher (one-time: reroll any test), Choir Bell (one-time: all Wayfarers clear 1 Stress)

**Monster Card Reference (Optional Advanced Play):**  
If playing with Monster cards, THE BELL SAINT emerges here instead of a simple Dread test. See Monster Deck section.

---

### Card 7: Frozen Pilgrims
**Type:** MORAL CHOICE  
**Dread Die:** d8

*Ten figures frozen mid-stride. Their faces are peaceful. Fresh ice, not ancient. These people were alive when this expedition began. Maybe yesterday. Maybe this morning.*

**No Test Required.** Choose one:

**A) Leave Them (No roll, no consequence)**
The expedition presses on. No Stress. Move to next room.

**B) Investigate (Cunning vs d8)**
On success: Gain 1 rare item and 1 Hope token. On failure: Gain 1 Stress and 1 Trauma (choose Wayfarer). Either way, mark **Haunted** in your journal—at the expedition's end, if you have 2+ Haunted marks, the final room changes.

**C) Attempt Rescue (Guard + Resolve combined roll, average the two highest dice vs d8)**
On success: These people thaw. They warn you: "The singing follows." All Wayfarers gain 1 Hope token. Gain no item. On failure: The ice fractures. Each Wayfarer gains 2 Stress, and one gains 1 Trauma.

---

### Card 8: Radioactive Nursery
**Type:** HAZARD  
**Dread Die:** d8

*Small bones. Small toys. Small shoes. All glowing faintly green. The air tastes wrong—not like poison, like absence.*

**Test:** Force or Cunning vs d8  
Highest roll counts.

**On Success:**
You move through quickly. Gain 1 item. Mark **Irradiated** on the expedition — this is spent after the next room (hostile or not).

**On Failure:**
Radiation clings. Each Wayfarer gains 1 Stress AND **Glowing** condition: next test by any Wayfarer rolls at -1 die step. Condition clears when that Wayfarer leads a successful test. Gain no item.

**Hazard Tag:** **Contaminated** — if the next room offers a choice to "clean up," that option costs 1 Hope token but clears Glowing from all Wayfarers.

---

### Card 9: The Corpse Orchard
**Type:** EXPLORATION  
**Dread Die:** d10

*Trees of bone, fruit of dark crystal. Beautiful in a way that hurts to look at. Something moves in the canopy.*

**Test:** Resolve or Cunning vs d10

**On Success:**
You gather carefully. Gain 2 items (draw 3, keep 2). One Wayfarer may clear 2 Stress. Mark **Harvest** — you may spend this at any future Failure to reduce Stress gained by 1.

**On Failure:**
The trees notice. Each Wayfarer gains 2 Stress. One Wayfarer gains 1 Trauma (choose who). Branches lash—all Wayfarers gain the **Wounded** condition (next test at -1 die step). Condition clears after 1 successful test by any Wayfarer.

**Loot:** Dark Fruit (one-time: reroll any test), Crystal Seed (passive: next Resolve test +1 die), Bone Saw (one-time: when you would gain Trauma, redirect it to Stress instead), Harvest Oil (clear 1 Stress for each Wayfarer)

---

## BAND 3: CLIMAX (Cards 10–16)
**Goal:** Sustained danger. Full boss encounter. Scars accumulate. Decisions feel impossible.

### Card 10: The Singing Pit
**Type:** HAZARD  
**Dread Die:** d10

*The echo from the Organ Choir has followed you. Here, it pools into a deep shaft. Something is singing back from below. Two voices. Harmonizing.*

**Test:** Resolve vs d10  
All Wayfarers roll; lowest roll counts.

**On Success (All Wayfarers Tie or Beat):**
You ignore the singing. Gain 1 item or 1 Hope token.

**On Success (Some Wayfarers Fail):**
Each Wayfarer that failed gains 1 Stress. Successful Wayfarers may redirect 1 of their Stress onto a failed Wayfarer's total.

**On Total Failure (All Wayfarers Fail):**
The singing reaches a crescendo. Each Wayfarer gains 2 Stress. One Wayfarer gains 1 Trauma (choose who). Mark **Echo** in the expedition: if another Singing or Resolve test appears in the next 4 cards, gain +1 Stress when you fail it.

---

### Card 11: THE BELL SAINT (FIRST BOSS)
**Type:** BOSS ENCOUNTER  
**Dread Die:** d12

*A figure of oxidized bronze and cracked bell-glass, impossibly tall. It has no face, only a seam where sound leaks out. Every step leaves ringing air.*

**This is a full combat encounter.** Use the Monster Card system OR resolve it as a single opposed test:

**Single Test Mode (Simpler for first play):**
Choose your champion. That Wayfarer rolls Force or Guard vs d12.

**On Success:**
The Bell Saint bows and recedes into shadow. All Wayfarers heal all Stress. Gain 2 rare items. One Wayfarer recovers 1 Trauma. Mark **Victory** in your journal.

**On Failure:**
The Bell Saint rings. Each Wayfarer gains 3 Stress. Up to 2 Wayfarers may each gain 1 Trauma instead of Stress. After the encounter, **the Bell Saint is marked as Wounded but Awake** — if you face 3+ more rooms, it may reappear (GM's choice or shuffle back into the deck as a 2-in-6 encounter).

**Boss Loot:** Saint Bell Relic (passive: one Wayfarer gains +1 Spirit permanently until expedition ends), Chiming Vial (one-time: all Wayfarers clear 2 Stress), Muted Shard (one-time: next Resolve test gets +2 dice), Bells of Passage (one-time: skip the next room, move directly to the card after)

---

### Card 12: Fractured Vestry
**Type:** CHOICE MOMENT  
**Dread Die:** d8

*The Bell Saint's aftermath. Bells lie broken across the floor, still resonating. Prayer books are scattered. Among them, something moves with intention.*

**No Test Required.** Choose one:

**A) Take the Prayer Books (Cunning vs d8)**
On success: Gain 1 item and mark **Blessed** — at any future Failure by another Wayfarer, you may erase Blessed to prevent 1 Stress. On failure: Gain 1 Stress and 1 Trauma.

**B) Search the Movement (Guard vs d8)**
On success: Gain a cryptic artifact (one-time: use at any point to add +2 to any test). Gain 1 Hope token. On failure: The thing takes form briefly—nightmarish. Each Wayfarer gains 1 Stress.

**C) Leave It Untouched (No roll, no consequence)**
Discipline. You move past. No Stress. But mark **Unfinished** — at the expedition's end, if you have 2+ Unfinished marks, a new room may be shuffled in.

---

### Card 13: Grave Humor
**Type:** EXPLORATION  
**Dread Die:** d8

*Crypts carved with figures in impossible positions. Some are laughing. It's been three hundred years. They're still mid-laugh.*

**Test:** Resolve vs d8

**On Success:**
Morbid treasure. Gain 2 items or mark **Dark Mirth** (at any future Failure, erase this to treat that Stress as Resolve instead, letting you clear it faster). One Wayfarer recovers 1 Stress.

**On Failure:**
The laughter gets worse. Each Wayfarer gains 1 Stress. One Wayfarer gains the **Disturbed** condition (next test by that Wayfarer rolls at -1 die step). Condition clears when that Wayfarer leads a successful test.

**Loot:** Skull Charm (one-time: reroll any test), Grave Dust (clear 1 Stress for each Wayfarer), Bone Dice (one-time: roll any test twice, use higher), Comedy Mask (passive: on any Failure, reduce Stress gained by 1)

---

### Card 14: The Stair Descends
**Type:** HAZARD  
**Dread Die:** d10

*Stone stairs, worn smooth. Descending forever. The air gets colder with each step. Behind you, you can hear stone grinding. The stairs are collapsing.*

**Test:** Guard vs d10  
All Wayfarers roll; if any Wayfarer beats the Dread Die, all Wayfarers count as successful.

**On Success (At Least One Beats):**
You outrun the collapse. Gain 1 item. No Stress.

**On Failure (All Tie or Lose):**
Each Wayfarer gains 1 Stress. The collapse reaches—one Wayfarer is **Buried** (they lose their next turn's Once-Per-Room ability and gain 1 Trauma instead of using it). After they lead the next successful test, they're freed.

**Hazard Tag:** **Pursued** — if a second Hazard appears before the end of the deck, it gains +1 Dread Die.

---

### Card 15: The Heart Chamber
**Type:** CHOICE MOMENT  
**Dread Die:** d10

*A vast hollow in the stone. In the center: something that was alive once, maybe still is. It pulses with bioluminescent light. The walls breathe in rhythm with it.*

**No Test Required.** Choose one:

**A) Approach the Heart (Resolve vs d10)**
On success: Place your hand on it. You see something true about the expedition. Gain 1 Hope token and 1 rare item. One Wayfarer recovers 2 Stress. On failure: The Heart rejects you. Gain 2 Stress each.

**B) Harvest from It (Cunning + Force combined roll, average highest vs d10)**
On success: Gain 2 rare items and 1 Hope token. One Wayfarer is **Marked by the Heart** (next hostile creature will single them out first, but they gain +1 die to Guard tests until marked elsewhere). On failure: It thrashes. Each Wayfarer gains 2 Stress.

**C) Destroy It (Force vs d10, with all Wayfarers' rolls averaged)**
On success: The chamber collapses safely. Gain 1 Hope token. No Stress. But mark **Desecration** — the final room changes, becomes harder. On failure: Collapse is not safe. Each Wayfarer gains 2 Stress. One Wayfarer gains 1 Trauma.

---

### Card 16: The Calling
**Type:** HAZARD  
**Dread Die:** d10

*Your name. All your names. Spoken in a voice that is not hostile, just insistent. The walls repeat it. The floor echoes it. It's coming from everywhere and nowhere.*

**Test:** Resolve vs d10  
All Wayfarers roll; highest roll counts.

**On Success:**
You answer with silence. The calling stops. Gain 1 item or 1 Hope token. Clear 1 Stress.

**On Failure:**
The calling gets personal. Each Wayfarer gains 2 Stress. One Wayfarer gains the **Answering** condition (at the end of the next room, if that Wayfarer has not led a successful test, they gain 1 Trauma). Condition clears after that Wayfarer leads a successful test.

**Hazard Tag:** **Haunted** — mark this. At the expedition's end, if 2+ Haunted cards have been played, the final room includes a ghost encounter (non-lethal but costly).

---

## BAND 4: ENDGAME (Cards 17–20)
**Goal:** Final gauntlet. Escape pressure. Last consequences matter.

### Card 17: The Warden's Gate
**Type:** CHOICE MOMENT  
**Dread Die:** d10

*A portal, half-open. Beyond it: light, noise, the world you remember. Here: the hand of something vast, imploring you not to leave.*

**No Test Required.** Choose one:

**A) Run Through (Guard vs d10)**
On success: You're halfway to freedom. Gain 1 Hope token. On failure: The hand catches one Wayfarer. That Wayfarer gains 1 Trauma. All others gain 1 Stress. The gate closes slightly—next room's Dread Die is +1.

**B) Negotiate with the Hand (Cunning vs d10)**
On success: It releases you. The next room is skipped (you move directly ahead). Gain 2 Hope tokens. On failure: It's angry now. Each Wayfarer gains 2 Stress. Mark **Pursued** — see card 14.

**C) Defy It (Force vs d10)**
On success: Spectacular. One Wayfarer becomes a legend. Gain 3 Hope tokens. That Wayfarer recovers all Stress. On failure: You anger it. Each Wayfarer gains 3 Stress. One gains 1 Trauma (choose who).

---

### Card 18: The Hall of Echoes
**Type:** EXPLORATION  
**Dread Die:** d10

*Every footstep multiplies. Every breath returns as a voice. You see yourself walking backward, your names spelled wrong, your fears made flesh in the stone.*

**Test:** Resolve or Cunning vs d10

**On Success:**
You recognize the trick. It's a mirror, not a prophecy. Gain 2 items. All Wayfarers clear 1 Stress. Mark **Clarity** — at the next Failure, erase this to prevent 1 Trauma.

**On Failure:**
The echoes congeal. Each Wayfarer gains 2 Stress. One Wayfarer gains 1 Trauma (choose who). That Wayfarer also gains the **Haunted** condition (their next test rolls at -1 die step). Condition clears after they lead a successful test.

**Loot:** Mirror Shard (one-time: see the next card before it's revealed), Echo Charm (passive: on any Failure, reduce Stress by 1), Silence Stone (one-time: all Wayfarers clear 2 Stress), Memory Vial (one-time: next Resolve or Cunning test gets +2 dice)

---

### Card 19: THE FINAL VIGIL (SECOND BOSS or Ritual Encounter)
**Type:** BOSS/RITUAL  
**Dread Die:** d12

*A figure stands at the expedition's threshold. It is made of everything you've lost here. It is every room that hurt. It is the shape of your regrets. It asks: "Will you leave anyone behind?"*

**This is the most dangerous encounter.** You may resolve it as a single combat or as a group Resolve test:

**Group Ritual Test (All Wayfarers together):**
All Wayfarers roll Resolve. Sum the highest 2 rolls and add any Hope tokens spent. Roll d12 as the Dread Die.

**On Success:**
The figure bows. It releases what it holds. All Wayfarers heal all Stress and recover 1 Trauma each. Gain 3 rare items total. Mark **Passage Granted**.

**On Failure:**
The figure reaches for one of you. Each Wayfarer gains 2 Stress. Up to 3 Wayfarers may each gain 1 Trauma instead of Stress. Mark **Owed** — at the expedition's end, the epilogue changes. If 3+ Wayfarers have "Owed" marks, the expedition failed even if you escape.

**Boss Loot:** Final Vigil Torch (passive: one Wayfarer gains +1 die to Resolve until expedition ends), Passage Seal (one-time: skip the next room), Regret Mirror (one-time: all Wayfarers clear 3 Stress), Vow Stone (one-time: at any Failure, prevent 2 Stress)

---

### Card 20: The Exit
**Type:** FINALE  
**Dread Die:** d12

*The way out. Light. The smell of open air. But the threshold is narrow, and something ancient guards it. Not evil. Just ancient. It asks: "What did you learn?"*

**Final Test (All Wayfarers):**
Each Wayfarer must contribute to one final roll. Choose: Force, Cunning, Resolve, or Guard. All Wayfarers roll one die from their chosen stat. Average the rolls (round up). Compare to d12.

**On Success:**
You emerge. You are scarred, changed, but you emerge. You carry what you learned. This is a victory. Count your Hope tokens and Passage marks.

**Ending Tiers:**
- **0-2 Hope tokens, no Passage Granted:** Bittersweet Exit. You survived. Many did not.
- **3-5 Hope tokens OR Passage Granted:** Triumphant Exit. You will tell this story.
- **6+ Hope tokens AND Passage Granted:** Legendary Exit. You return changed, worthy, ready to go back.
- **3+ Owed marks, any Hope:** Pyrrhic Exit. You escaped, but at what cost?

**On Failure:**
The ancient thing reaches. One final Wayfarer gains 1 Trauma. But the doors open anyway. You stumble into light, exhausted. This is not victory, but it is survival. The scars will teach you later what you missed today.

## Pacing Notes

**Run Length:**
- Fast: 10 cards (skip cards 12, 14, 16, 18, 20) = 45 minutes
- Standard: 20 cards (full deck) = 90–120 minutes
- Slow/Narrative: 20 cards + monster cards = 2+ hours

**Difficulty Scaling:**
- Wayfarers begin soft (Bone Gate, Flooded Chapel).
- Pressure hits at The Organ Choir (Card 6).
- Boss at Card 11. If they fail badly, Card 14 is gentler.
- Second boss at Card 19. This is the final check.
- Card 20 resolves the entire expedition.

**Stress Economy:**
- Average Wayfarer can take 6–7 Stress before Trauma.
- Boss fights grant significant Stress/Trauma.
- Hope tokens and item abilities are your relief valves.
- If the whole group is near Trauma limit by Card 15, Card 19 becomes lethal.

## Optional: Monster Card Integration

If you want to use dedicated Monster cards instead of Dread Die tests:

**Card 6 (The Organ Choir):** The Bell Saint appears as a full combat card.
**Card 11 (The Bell Saint):** Same as Card 6 (reuse the card or shuffle it from the encounter deck).
**Card 19 (The Final Vigil):** The Vigil Entity appears—similar to The Bell Saint but with different loot.

Monster cards have:
- Name
- Dread Die
- Stress value (how much it inflicts per round)
- Start-of-round ability
- Attack behavior (roll vs. stat)
- Special mechanic
- Mutation interaction
- Loot drop

## First Playtest Notes

1. **Print these 20 cards in order.** Shuffle only if you want chaos.
2. **Use one Wayfarer per player.** Solo players pick 2 Wayfarers and roleplay both.
3. **Resolve failures fast.** Don't stall on narrative—gain Stress and move.
4. **Let Hope tokens accumulate.** They're your relief. Use them.
5. **At Card 11 or 19, if the group is in bad shape, offer a "strategic Trauma trade"** — trade 2 Stress for 1 Trauma. This keeps the game tense, not grinding.
6. **The ending changes with Hope and Passage markers.** This is how a full expedition feels different on replay.

**You're ready to print.**
