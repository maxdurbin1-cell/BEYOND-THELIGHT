# Card Expedition: Mutation Cards (12 Cards)

## What Are Mutations?

**Mutations** are permanent, character-altering tweaks that change how you play AND how you roleplay.

- Mutations are **drafted before the expedition starts** (or drawn randomly).
- Each Wayfarer picks 1 mutation before turn 1.
- Mutations persist for the entire expedition.
- Multiple Wayfarers can have the same mutation.
- Mutations affect your stats, roleplay, and how NPCs/creatures treat you.

**Print these 12 cards. Shuffle before the expedition. Each player draws 1 mutation and keeps it face-up in front of their Wayfarer card.**

---

## MUTATION CARDS (12 Total)

### Mutation 1: Glass Teeth

**Visual:** Mouth of jagged crystalline shards.

**Mechanics:**
- **+1 Force die** on your next Strike test.
- Your Guard die is **-1 step** (people are wary, you're visibly dangerous).

**Roleplay:**
- You cannot eat normally. You subsist on liquids and soft foods.
- When you speak, there's an unsettling acoustic quality.
- Creatures react with instinctive fear or respect—your choice of presentation.

**Interaction:**
- At any social test (Cunning with an NPC), you may choose: intimidate (+1 die) or unsettle (they don't trust you, but they obey).

---

### Mutation 2: Choir Lungs

**Visual:** Your breath echoes in unnatural harmonics.

**Mechanics:**
- **Resolve tests get +1 die** when you're singing or making sound.
- You **cannot be silenced or gagged** (you always have a voice, literally).
- You **attract attention**. Any stealth test takes a -1 die penalty.

**Roleplay:**
- You hear echoes of the Organ Choir in your chest. Their song never fully left.
- When you breathe, it resonates in rooms. When you speak, it carries further.
- You are compelled to hum or sing at tense moments (you can't help it).

**Interaction:**
- At any moment, you may make a **Resolve test vs d8** to activate the Choir's Song: all Wayfarers gain +1 die on their next test, but you gain 1 Stress. Once per expedition.

---

### Mutation 3: Third Arm

**Visual:** A third limb, vestigial but functional, wraps around your torso or shoulder.

**Mechanics:**
- **+1 item slot** (you can hold one extra item).
- **Force tests get +1 die** when carrying heavy objects or climbing.
- **Guard tests get -1 die** (your armor doesn't fit right; the extra arm breaks your balance).

**Roleplay:**
- Your clothes are custom-made or torn to accommodate the limb.
- You're ambidextrous and can do two delicate tasks at once.
- Some people are fascinated; some are horrified. Both reactions are common.

**Interaction:**
- Once per room, when you would drop an item, you may instead juggle it between your three limbs and keep it. No test required.

---

### Mutation 4: Bone Sight

**Visual:** Your eyes are solid white, veins of bone running through them.

**Mechanics:**
- **Cunning tests get +1 die** when perceiving hidden objects, traps, or secrets.
- You can see in complete darkness (no light needed).
- **You cannot look away from death.** When another Wayfarer gains Trauma, you perceive it vividly and gain 1 Stress yourself.

**Roleplay:**
- You are drawn to bones, death sites, and ancient places.
- You see the "bones" of architecture—load-bearing walls, weak points, the skeleton beneath.
- You are deeply unsettled by things that defy skeletal logic (flesh without bone, soft things that should be hard).

**Interaction:**
- Before entering a new room, you may make a **Cunning test vs d8** to sense what "bones" lie ahead. On success, describe one major structural feature or hazard. Costs no action.

---

### Mutation 5: Radiant Scar

**Visual:** A glowing line of radiation scars across your skin, pulsing faintly green.

**Mechanics:**
- **Resolve tests get +1 die** when facing radiation or toxic environments.
- You emit faint light (dim, not bright; useful for reading small text in darkness).
- **Cunning and Guard tests take -1 die** (the glow gives away your position).

**Roleplay:**
- You glow brighter when stressed or emotional.
- You are slowly, visibly poisoning yourself. You know this. You don't care (or you've stopped caring).
- You are attracted to other radiant places and crave irradiated food/water (it feels like "home").

**Interaction:**
- Once per expedition, you may **overdrive your scar** and step up your Force die for one test. Afterward, gain 1 Trauma immediately. Breaking your body for power.

---

### Mutation 6: Fractured Mind

**Visual:** Your thoughts leak audibly—whispers, contradictions, phantom voices.

**Mechanics:**
- **Resolve tests get +1 die** (you are used to fighting your own will).
- Any test with **Cunning gets -1 die** (you can't focus; too many voices).
- You **cannot lie convincingly.** When you try to deceive someone, they get +1 die to see through you.

**Roleplay:**
- You are barely holding yourself together.
- You sometimes answer questions no one asked.
- You speak in fragments. Other Wayfarers finish your sentences.
- You are paranoid about your own choices—were they yours?

**Interaction:**
- Once per room, when you fail a Resolve test, you may instead succeed—but the "other voice" takes over for the next test (GM controls your action). You regain control after.

---

### Mutation 7: Stone Skin

**Visual:** Your flesh has patches of grey, ossified stone—rough, hard, beautiful.

**Mechanics:**
- **Guard tests get +1 die** (stone absorbs impact).
- **Force tests get -1 die** (stone is heavy; you're slower).
- When you would take damage, you may convert up to 1 Trauma to Stress instead (stone doesn't feel pain). Once per expedition.

**Roleplay:**
- You are becoming less human, more statue.
- You move slowly, deliberately. You are patient.
- Warm places feel wrong. You seek cool stone and caves.
- You are afraid of being buried alive—and also drawn to it.

**Interaction:**
- When any Wayfarer is in an enclosed space and panicking, you gain +1 die on Resolve tests (the stone remembers the earth).

---

### Mutation 8: Bleeding Hands

**Visual:** Your hands perpetually bleed black ichor that smells of copper and old grief.

**Mechanics:**
- **Strike and Force tests get +1 die** (you've accepted the blood; it flows through you).
- **Cunning tests take -1 die** (blood drips everywhere; you can't hide).
- Once per room, when you touch something, you may learn its "history" by bleeding on it (GM describes one fact about its past). No test required.

**Roleplay:**
- Your hands are stained permanently.
- Everything you touch gets marked with blood.
- You grieve for things you've never met.
- Other Wayfarers are superstitious around your touch (is it contagious?).

**Interaction:**
- Bleeding hands can "seal" broken things temporarily by pressing them together with blood. Lasts one room. Once per expedition.

---

### Mutation 9: Echo Heart

**Visual:** Your chest is hollow. Your heartbeat echoes, audible to everyone nearby.

**Mechanics:**
- **Resolve tests get +1 die** (your heart is loud; you trust it more than your mind).
- **Stealth / Cunning tests take -1 die** (your heartbeat gives away position).
- You can sense when other Wayfarers are near death (heartbeat falters, slows). You know when they gain Trauma.

**Roleplay:**
- Your heartbeat is your truth. You can't hide what you feel.
- You are deeply empathetic. You cry easily.
- You trust gut feelings over logic.
- You are exhausted by the vulnerability of an audible heart.

**Interaction:**
- Once per room, when another Wayfarer is about to fail a Resolve test, you may **lend them your heartbeat**: they get +1 die, but you gain 1 Stress.

---

### Mutation 10: Crowbound

**Visual:** Spectral crows perch on your shoulders. They're not quite real—see-through, smoking, hungry.

**Mechanics:**
- **Cunning tests get +1 die** (the crows see everything).
- When you're alone, you gain -1 die on all tests (the crows demand attention).
- You must feed the crows: once per day, you need to spend an item or gain 1 Stress (they're always hungry).

**Roleplay:**
- The crows whisper secrets and lies in equal measure.
- They are jealous of your attention to other Wayfarers.
- They remember things you wish you'd forget.
- You speak to them like they're real. They probably are.

**Interaction:**
- You may send the crows to scout ahead. Make a **Cunning test vs d8**. On success, describe one detail about the next room before it's revealed.

---

### Mutation 11: Drowning Memory

**Visual:** Water constantly weeps from your eyes, your mouth, your pores. You smell like the deep.

**Mechanics:**
- **Resolve and Cunning tests get +1 die** in water or wet environments.
- **Force tests take -1 die** (everything feels heavy, like drowning).
- You can breathe underwater. You cannot breathe air without effort (it's dry, painful).

**Roleplay:**
- You are grieving something you drowned in.
- You are compelled toward water and depths.
- You leave wet footprints everywhere.
- You speak slowly, as if pushing words through liquid.

**Interaction:**
- Once per expedition, you may **return to the deep** and abandon the expedition temporarily. You escape one room's consequences entirely. You rejoin the next room, fully healed of Stress. But if you do this at Card 20, you don't reach the exit (you drowned choosing the depths).

---

### Mutation 12: Stigmata

**Visual:** Ritual scars cover your body in patterns you don't remember making.

**Mechanics:**
- **All stat tests get +1 die** when you lean into pain (choose to suffer).
- You automatically gain 1 Stress at the start of each room (the scars itch, remind you).
- You can **sense lies**. When someone lies to you (NPC or Wayfarer), your scars burn and you know. But you can't prove it.

**Roleplay:**
- You are devoted to something. You don't remember what.
- You mark yourself further throughout the expedition.
- You are both self-destructive and self-flagellating.
- You believe suffering is penance for a sin you can't recall.

**Interaction:**
- Once per room, when you fail a test, you may **open a scar intentionally** and re-roll. If you fail again, you gain 1 Trauma (severe wound rupture). Once per expedition, you may open all scars at once for +2 to any roll, but gain 2 Trauma immediately.

## Mutation Integration Notes

### With Wayfarers:
- **Jax + Glass Teeth** = Scary protector (intimidating presence)
- **Ena + Bone Sight** = Scout who sees secrets
- **Brother Cal + Stigmata** = Self-sacrificing martyr
- **Mira + Bleeding Hands** = Desperate scavenger who pays in blood

### With Chapter Cards:
- **Bone Gate (Card 1):** Bone Sight triggers early.
- **Flooded Chapel (Card 2):** Drowning Memory gains +1 die.
- **Radioactive Nursery (Card 8):** Radiant Scar is immune to Glowing condition.
- **The Organ Choir (Card 6):** Choir Lungs recognizes kinship.
- **The Heart Chamber (Card 15):** Radiant Scar, Stone Skin, Stigmata all interact with the Heart.

### Mutation Stacking (Advanced Play):
If 2+ Wayfarers have mutations that interact, you may combine effects:
- **Glass Teeth + Bleeding Hands:** On a successful Strike, add narrative flourish (broken teeth, blood trails).
- **Bone Sight + Echo Heart:** You sense nearby death and dying things.
- **Choir Lungs + Crowbound:** The crows sing in harmony with your lungs.

---

## First Playtest: Mutation Selection

Before the expedition starts, each player:
1. Draws a random mutation (or picks one).
2. Reads it aloud.
3. Describes how it manifests on their Wayfarer (visually, aurally, spiritually).
4. Keeps the card visible throughout the expedition.

**Mutations should feel like permanent reminders that this expedition changed you before it even started.**
