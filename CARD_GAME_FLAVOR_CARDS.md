# Card Expedition: Personal Flavor Cards (8 Cards)

## What Are Personal Flavor Cards?

**Personal Flavor cards** are one-time, character-specific powers that feel more like **emotional moments** than mechanical bonuses.

- Each Wayfarer gets **2 Personal Flavor cards** (8 total, 2 per Wayfarer).
- These are drawn at the start of the expedition or held until needed.
- Once used, the card is discarded permanently.
- Unlike Break abilities, these are **thematic to that character's story**, not just mechanical.

**Print these 8 cards. Distribute 2 to each Wayfarer player at the start. They can use them at any moment during the expedition (even mid-roll). You get to decide when your character's moment arrives.**

---

## JAX MERIDIAN'S PERSONAL FLAVOR CARDS

### Jax Flavor 1: Sworn Shield

**Type:** Protective Oath

*"Not one more. I swear it."*

**When to Use:**
Before any Wayfarer would gain Trauma.

**Effect:**
- That Wayfarer does not gain the Trauma.
- Instead, Jax gains 1 Trauma on their behalf.
- Read this aloud: *"I've survived worse. Let me carry this one."*
- After using this, Jax cannot use his Break ability (he's already sacrificed enough).

**Why This Matters:**
Jax's whole identity is *Shared Burden*. This is him living it. He can only do it twice per expedition (once here, once with his Break), so choose wisely.

**Art Direction:** Jax stepping between another Wayfarer and shadow.

---

### Jax Flavor 2: Iron Vigil

**Type:** Last Stand

*"I will hold this ground."*

**When to Use:**
During any Hazard card when the group is about to fail collectively.

**Effect:**
- Jax alone faces the Hazard (solo test vs the Dread Die) instead of the group.
- If Jax beats the test, the entire group succeeds (no Stress for anyone).
- If Jax fails, he alone takes all the Stress that the group would have taken (doubled).
- This is a solo commitment—no help, no other abilities stack.

**Why This Matters:**
Sometimes Jax stands alone so others don't have to fall. This is the fantasy of the protector.

**Art Direction:** Jax alone facing a descending darkness.

## ENA DRIFT'S PERSONAL FLAVOR CARDS

### Ena Flavor 1: The Veil Thins

**Type:** Prescience

*"The room already told me its secrets."*

**When to Use:**
When the next Chapter card is about to be revealed.

**Effect:**
- Flip the next card face-down and read it silently (without revealing to other players).
- Describe ONE true detail about the room aloud to warn the group.
- The group can then prepare strategy based on your warning.
- Then reveal the card normally.

**Why This Matters:**
Ena's gift is *reading rooms*. This lets her literally do it before anyone else can react. It's a gift and a burden—she sees danger coming and has to decide what to warn about.

**Art Direction:** Ena's silhouette against a translucent Chapter card.

---

### Ena Flavor 2: Ghost Step Refrain

**Type:** Second Chance Echo

*"I've already walked this path. I know where it leads."*

**When to Use:**
After you fail any test (your test, not another Wayfarer's).

**Effect:**
- Reroll that test immediately.
- You must use the new result, even if it's worse.
- You do NOT gain Stress for using this (unlike your normal Once-Per-Room Ghost Step).
- But mark it: the next time you reroll, you must accept +1 Stress.

**Why This Matters:**
Ena gets a free insurance policy once. But it comes with a debt (the next reroll costs more).

**Art Direction:** Ena stepping backward through a ghostly echo of herself.

## BROTHER CAL ASH'S PERSONAL FLAVOR CARDS

### Brother Cal Flavor 1: Benediction of the Dust

**Type:** Final Mercy

*"Go in peace. I will remain."*

**When to Use:**
When a Wayfarer reaches 5 Trauma (one step before death).

**Effect:**
- That Wayfarer is healed to 3 Trauma (they recover 2 Trauma).
- Brother Cal gains 1 Trauma immediately (he carries their burden).
- Read aloud: *"The Dust Saints remember you. You will not fall alone."*
- After using this, Brother Cal's passive ability (Shared Burden) does not work until the next Chapter card (he's exhausted from the prayer).

**Why This Matters:**
Brother Cal is all about **sacrifice for others**. This is his ultimate mercy—he saves someone from the brink by paying dearly himself.

**Art Direction:** Brother Cal laying his hands on a dying Wayfarer, ash rising.

---

### Brother Cal Flavor 2: Rite of Safe Passage

**Type:** Pilgrimage Ward

*"This place is holy ground for one moment. It will not touch us."*

**When to Use:**
Before entering a room (any Chapter card).

**Effect:**
- You may **skip all Stress gains from this one room** (failure consequences don't apply).
- You still gain Trauma if the card would cause it (you can't escape true cost).
- All other consequences (conditions, markers) still apply.
- After using this, Brother Cal must mark **Spent Faith** (his next Resolve test takes -1 die until he leads a successful test).

**Why This Matters:**
Brother Cal bought a reprieve. But faith doesn't grow back instantly. He pays spiritually.

**Art Direction:** Brother Cal with hands raised, a glowing circle protecting the group from approaching darkness.

## MIRA VALE'S PERSONAL FLAVOR CARDS

### Mira Flavor 1: Jackpot

**Type:** Desperate Salvage

*"Everything here has value. I just need to find the right buyer."*

**When to Use:**
During any room where you gain items OR after a Failure (your choice of timing).

**Effect:**
- Draw 3 items from the loot pool (instead of the usual 1–2).
- Keep all 3, even if you exceed your item slots.
- Your item slots are increased by 1 until you use or discard one of these items.
- Mark **Debt**: the next time you gain an item freely, lose 1 instead (the salvage broker's tax).

**Why This Matters:**
Mira is the loot engine. This is her moment of maximum profiteering. But like all her schemes, it comes with strings.

**Art Direction:** Mira surrounded by glowing loot, hands full, grinning.

---

### Mira Flavor 2: Bone Market Miracle

**Type:** Resurrection Deal

*"I know someone. Someone who owes me. I'm calling in the favor."*

**When to Use:**
When a Wayfarer would gain 1 Trauma (any Trauma source).

**Effect:**
- That Trauma is prevented.
- Instead, search the **item discard pile** (items used or discarded in this expedition).
- Take one item from the discard and put it into play for the affected Wayfarer.
- All other Wayfarers gain 1 Stress (the "favor" unbalances things spiritually).

**Why This Matters:**
Mira has connections. In the underworld, debts and deals are currency. This is her calling in a favor from someone unseen. But favors have ripples.

**Art Direction:** Mira making a gesture to invisible forces, shadows coalescing into an item.

## HOW TO USE PERSONAL FLAVOR CARDS

### Before the Expedition:
1. Each player receives their Wayfarer's 2 Personal Flavor cards.
2. Read them aloud to the group so everyone knows what's available.
3. Keep them in front of you (visible, not hidden).

### During Play:
- You may use a Personal Flavor card **at any time**, even mid-roll.
- You don't need to "save" them for a specific moment (though you can).
- Once used, the card is discarded permanently.
- You don't get replacements; you get exactly 2 per character, per expedition.

### Strategic Timing:
- Some cards work best as **insurance** (hold until desperate).
- Some cards work best as **enablers** (use early to set up your character's fantasy).
- Talk about when you want to use them. It's not cheating to coordinate with your group.

---

## FLAVOR CARD INTERACTIONS

### Stacking with Abilities:
- Personal Flavor cards **don't count as Break abilities** (you still have your Break available).
- Personal Flavor cards **can stack with Once-Per-Room abilities** (use both if needed).
- Personal Flavor cards **count as "using" a resource**, so some may prevent future ability use (read the card).

### With Mutations:
- **Glass Teeth + Jax's Sworn Shield:** Jax protecting another Wayfarer feels earned—his teeth are literally sacrificial.
- **Echo Heart + Brother Cal's Benediction:** Brother Cal senses the Wayfarer's dying heart and acts.
- **Drowning Memory + Mira's Jackpot:** Mira salvages value even from drowning.

### With Items:
- Personal Flavor cards are **different from Items**. You can use both in the same room.
- Items are tactical (reroll, clear Stress). Flavor cards are thematic (story moments).

## THEMATIC INTENT

Each Flavor card should feel like **a character's defining moment in miniature**:

- **Jax:** "I stand between you and the dark."
- **Ena:** "I already know what's coming."
- **Brother Cal:** "I will carry what you cannot."
- **Mira:** "There's always a deal to be made."

Use them to **reinforce who your Wayfarer is**, not just to solve mechanical problems.

When you use a Personal Flavor card, read it aloud and roleplay the moment. Let it linger.

---

## FIRST PLAYTEST: FLAVOR CARD MOMENTS

During your first expedition, look for these moments:

- **Jax saves someone from Trauma** (Sworn Shield) — Remember this moment. It's why Jax exists.
- **Ena warns the group before they enter danger** (The Veil Thins) — Discuss how her prescience changed your strategy.
- **Brother Cal holds the line when all seems lost** (Benediction of the Dust) — This is his prayer made manifest.
- **Mira makes a desperate deal** (Jackpot or Bone Market Miracle) — Someone off-screen paid for this favor. Who? Why?

These are your **story beats**. Treasure them.

---

## MECHANICAL BALANCE NOTE

Personal Flavor cards are **deliberately unbalanced**:

- **Jax's cards** are about **protection** (preventing Trauma, holding ground).
- **Ena's cards** are about **information and rerolls** (knowledge advantage, do-overs).
- **Brother Cal's cards** are about **healing and reprieve** (Trauma recovery, Stress avoidance).
- **Mira's cards** are about **loot and resurrection** (more items, second chances through salvage).

Each Wayfarer's Flavor cards should **feel uniquely powerful to their playstyle**. That's intentional.

If someone feels overpowered, remember: **Personal Flavor cards are limited**. You get exactly 2. Once spent, they're gone. This creates natural scarcity.

If you want to add more Flavor cards or customize them for your group:

**Template:**

```
[WAYFARER NAME] — [FLAVOR TITLE]

Type: [Protective / Prescient / Healing / Salvage]

Tagline: "[A thematic 1-line quote]"

When to Use:
[Specific trigger or timing]

Effect:
[What happens when you use this]
[Consequences or side effects]

Why This Matters:
[1-2 sentences explaining the thematic resonance]
```

Use this for house rules or expansions after your first playtest.
